An entry for the [One Word From Grandma](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-word-from-grandma-f04bed16) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun), where a judge model compares words two at a time and the funniest, most grandma-like word wins.

The program writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`:

> **ROBIT**

The typo a grandma actually makes typing "robot" — warm, a little baffled,
funny in a single word, tied to machines, and fresh against the obvious
"robot". Five bytes, no trailing newline, so the smaller-text tie-break
works in its favour.
